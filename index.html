For organisers

Your class deserves a full room.

Yoga teachers, tutors, run-club leads, potters, guitar teachers, game-night hosts. List it once and stop living in group chats.

  • List in minutes. A one-off event or a weekly class with its own schedule.
  • Share one link. Drop it in every group you’re already in.
  • A real headcount. Neighbours register free, so you know who’s coming.
  • Check people in at the door. Scan their QR ticket.
  • Keep your regulars close. Post announcements to your community and see how your listings are doing.

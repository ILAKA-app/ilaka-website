Discover what's happening in your neighbourhood.

Ilaka is a hyperlocal platform to discover and register for local events, classes, and workshops — from yoga and dance to tutoring and community meetups, all near you.

Who it's for

For participants

Browse events and classes happening near your locality, filtered by your interests. Register and pay securely in a few taps, and get a digital ticket instantly.

For organisers

Event organisers, tutors, coaches, and class instructors can list their offerings, manage registrations, and get paid — with an analytics dashboard to track performance.

How it works

1

Discover

Find events and classes near you, matched to your interests and locality.

2

Register

Pick a listing and register in a few taps.

3

Pay

Pay the organiser's registration fee securely through the platform.

4

Get your QR ticket

Receive a digital QR-coded ticket instantly, saved in your account for entry.

Ilaka is free for everyone who attends an event or class. Organisers pay a small registration fee percentage and optional subscription/promotion fees — see Pricing.

1. Nature of the service

Ilaka operates a two-sided online marketplace connecting event organisers, tutors, coaches, and class instructors ("Organisers") with individuals seeking to discover, register for, and attend events and classes ("Users"). Ilaka acts as an intermediary platform facilitating discovery, registration, payment collection, and digital ticketing. Ilaka is not the organiser of any listed event or class and is not a party to the underlying agreement between a User and an Organiser for the event or class itself.

2. Registration fees, collection, and settlement

Where an event or class requires payment, Ilaka collects the registration fee from the User on the Organiser's behalf through its designated payment partner. Ilaka retains a platform transaction fee (currently 10–12% of the registration amount) before settling the net amount to the Organiser. Settlement to Organisers is processed on a T+1 basis for fully KYC-verified Organisers and a T+2 basis for new or unverified Organisers, where T is the date of the event or class session. See our Refund & Cancellation Policy for how cancellations affect settlement.

3. Organiser subscription bands

Organisers may be charged a monthly subscription fee determined by a band indexed to their active roster — the greater of the total membership of communities they own on the Platform and the number of distinct users who registered for their listings in the trailing twelve months. Current bands and fees are published on our Pricing page. Organisers with an active roster of 10 or fewer are not charged a subscription.

Band changes are notified to the Organiser in advance through the Platform. An increase in band takes effect from the following billing cycle and never retrospectively; a decrease takes effect immediately. An Organiser who disputes their roster count may raise it with us at support@ilaka.co.in for review. Ilaka may change published band thresholds or fees on reasonable prior notice; continued use after the notice period constitutes acceptance.

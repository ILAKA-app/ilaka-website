Delete Your Account

How to request deletion of your Ilaka account and personal data.

What deletion means

When you request account deletion, we:

  • Delete your profile and personal data (name, mobile number, locality, activity preferences).
  • Remove your community memberships.
  • Dissociate — but do not destroy — financial records (registrations, payments, invoices), which we're required to retain for a period under RBI and GST regulations. These are kept in a form no longer linked to your identity.
You can also start this from inside the Ilaka app, under Profile → Delete Account, once that's available in your app version. Until then, the request below works the same way.

How to request deletion

1

Email support@ilaka.co.in with the subject "Account Deletion Request", from the email or with the mobile number your account is registered under.

2

We verify the request against your registered mobile number to confirm it's really you.

3

We process the deletion and reply once it's done, per the DPDP Act, 2023.

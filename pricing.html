Pricing

Free for everyone who attends. Organisers pay a small, transparent fee to list, collect payments, and grow their reach.

Free for users

Ilaka is entirely free for end users. There are no platform charges, subscription fees, or hidden costs for people discovering and attending events or classes. The only payment a user makes is the registration fee for a specific event or class, which is set by the organiser and collected by Ilaka on the organiser's behalf.

Organiser subscription bands

The monthly subscription is indexed to the size of the organiser's active roster, not charged as a flat tier. A flat fee is simultaneously a deterrent to the smallest organisers and a substantial under-recovery from the largest. Banding holds the fee within roughly 0.5–1.0% of the organiser's own gross receipts at every size, and organisers move between bands automatically as their roster grows — no renegotiation.

Active roster is the greater of (a) the total membership of communities the organiser owns on the platform, and (b) the number of distinct users who registered for any of that organiser's listings in the trailing twelve months. Both are aggregate counts already held by the platform — no identifiable participant data is used in, or disclosed by, this calculation.

Band changes are notified to the organiser in advance. An increase takes effect from the following billing cycle; a decrease takes effect immediately.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 July 2026

Ilaka ("we", "us") is committed to protecting the personal data of everyone who uses the Ilaka platform, in compliance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act).

1. What we collect

To provide the Platform, we collect: your name, mobile number, location, and activity preferences (the categories of events and classes you're interested in).

2. Consent

We obtain your explicit, informed consent during onboarding, before collecting any personal data. You may withdraw your consent and request deletion of your personal data at any time through a clearly accessible mechanism in the app.

3. How we use your data

Your data is used to personalise your event and class recommendations, determine locally relevant listings, and facilitate your registrations and payments.

4. Sharing with organisers

We do not share your data in identifiable form with any third party, including event organisers. Organisers only receive aggregated, anonymised demographic insights about the people registering for their listings — never your individual identity or contact details beyond what's necessary for entry verification (e.g. your registration/ticket record).

5. Payment data

Payments are processed by a licensed Payment Aggregator regulated by the Reserve Bank of India (Razorpay). Ilaka does not store your card, UPI, or bank account credentials — these are handled directly by our payment partner in accordance with RBI guidelines.

6. Your rights

You have the right to access, correct, and request deletion of your personal data, and to withdraw consent at any time. See Delete Your Account for how to request deletion, or contact us — see Contact Us.

7. Data retention

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to provide the Platform's services and to meet legal and regulatory obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be communicated in-app.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or requests to exercise your data rights can be directed to us via our Contact Us page.
